## Privacy edits

The original captures contain personal wallpaper and a private network label. They remain local and are excluded from Git. Public copies were edited with ImageGen to replace the photographic background with an illustrative landscape inspired by Ghent, Belgium, and the network name with `Wi-Fi`, including private content visible through translucent panels. The wallpaper depicts a generated interpretation of the Graslei/Korenlei riverfront; it is not an unedited travel photograph or part of the original TV capture.

These are AI-assisted privacy edits of recorded views, not unmodified, pixel-identical TV screenshots. They were visually compared with their source captures for the shown interface layout, application logos, focus state, and menu entries. Small rendering differences can remain. Use them to understand the interface, not to establish pixel fidelity or physical-test acceptance.

No application, firmware, startup, privacy, or recovery behavior was changed to prepare these assets. Editing a capture does not validate a feature, application login, playback, startup, or recovery. The [recorded status](../status.md) remains authoritative for those limits.

The JPEGs keep the source views' widescreen composition, contain no original photographs, and are embedded with relative paths and descriptive alt text. They were exported at JPEG quality 90, then optimized losslessly as progressive JPEGs with metadata removed. The hero shows the closed menu and focused Live TV card; the second image shows the input menu. No historical capture collection is published.
